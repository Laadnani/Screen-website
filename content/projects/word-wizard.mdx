## The idea

A small word-guessing game. The app picks a random five-letter word, shows a hint, and you get five tries to find it. After each guess, the tiles show which letters are right.

## What I built

- **Game logic in one Provider.** Picks the word and its hint, tracks tries left and the active row, and checks each guess letter by letter.
- **Reactive tile rows.** Each row of tiles responds to typing, and the focus moves down as you use up your tries.
- **Word lists in English and French**, about 200 English and 100 French words, each paired with a hint.
- **Monetisation done properly.** Banner and rewarded ads through Google Mobile Ads, with Google's consent form (UMP), so ads are only personalised when the player agrees.

## Results

A working Android prototype. It was my first full Flutter game loop, from state management to ads and consent, and it taught me how much of a release is configuration: Gradle versions, ad units and consent flows.
